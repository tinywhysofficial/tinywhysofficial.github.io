Privacy Policy: Tiny Whys Uploader

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Tiny Whys Uploader ("the app") is a private, internal tool operated by the owner of the Tiny Whys YouTube channel. Its only purpose is to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails that we create ourselves to our own YouTube channel, and to schedule when they are published.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information (youtube.readonly) so it can confirm which channel it is uploading to. The app does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and is used only to upload our own videos. We do not collect, sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties. The app is not used by, and does not collect information from, members of the public or viewers of the channel.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the Tiny Whys channel owner via the email address listed on the channel's About page.
