Tiny Whys

Tiny Whys is a YouTube channel that answers one big "why" question every day with two paper-cut characters: Pip, a curious kid, and Dot, a tiny glowing spark who explains how the world works.

Tiny Whys Uploader is a private tool used only by the Tiny Whys team to upload our own finished episodes to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data.

Watch the channel on YouTube: search for "Tiny Whys".
